| Platform | Semantic richness | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mastodon | low | free text profile, tags, property fields |
| LinkedIn | medium | structured skills, proprietary |
| Credly / OpenBadges platforms | high | verifiable credentials |
| ORCID | high | research-oriented profiles |

## Compatibility notes

### Mastodon

Good for federation, not for deep semantics.

### Solid

Best fit for semantic portability, but too niche to assume as a baseline.

### OpenBadges

Best fit for portable skills and credentials.

### Wikidata / SKOS

Best fit for interest taxonomies and stable concept IDs.

### Schema.org

Best fit for broad web compatibility.

## Gap in the market

There is no mainstream consumer platform that exports:

- structured interests
- knowledge depth
- values
- skills
- persona

in one interoperable profile.
